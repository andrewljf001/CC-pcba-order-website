Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 2026

PCBAForge ("we", "us", or "our") operates pcbaforge.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you:

  • Submit an inquiry or order (name, email, company, WhatsApp number)
  • Create an account (email address, password)
  • Upload files for quotation (Gerber, BOM, placement files)
  • Contact us via email or WhatsApp

We also automatically collect certain technical data including IP address, browser type, and pages visited for analytics and security purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process your PCBA orders and send you quotes
  • To communicate order status, tracking, and payment information
  • To verify your email address and secure your account
  • To improve our website and services
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage

Your personal data and order information are stored in Cloudflare D1 (a globally distributed database) and Cloudflare R2 (file storage). Your uploaded files (Gerber, BOM, etc.) are stored securely and only accessible to our engineering team for the purpose of processing your order.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data with:

  • Cloudflare — infrastructure and CDN provider
  • PayPal / PingPong — for payment processing (only what is necessary to complete the transaction)
  • DHL / FedEx — shipping carriers (name, address, phone)

5. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies necessary for session management and security. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. By continuing to use our website, you consent to our use of essential cookies.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Object to processing of your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at admin@pcbaforge.com.

7. Data Retention

We retain your order data for a minimum of 3 years for business and legal purposes. Account data is retained until you request deletion. Uploaded design files are retained for 1 year after order completion, then deleted.

8. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, secure password hashing, and encrypted storage of sensitive configuration data. However, no system is 100% secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please contact us at: admin@pcbaforge.com
