Shipping Policy
Last updated: May 2026

📦 We ship worldwide via DHL Express and FedEx International Priority. Shipping fees are quoted after production is complete, based on actual package weight and destination.

1. Production Lead Time

Before your order ships, it goes through our full production process:

ServiceTypical Lead Time
PCB Fabrication only5–7 business days
PCB + SMT Assembly7–12 business days
Full Turnkey + Testing10–18 business days

Lead times begin after payment is confirmed and all required files are received. Complex boards or high-layer-count PCBs may require additional time.

2. Shipping Carriers & Transit Time

CarrierServiceTransit Time
DHL ExpressWorldwide Express3–7 business days
FedExInternational Priority3–7 business days
OtherOn request (economy)7–20 business days

Transit times are estimates and may vary due to customs clearance, holidays, or carrier delays outside our control.

3. Shipping Cost

Shipping fees are not included in your initial quote. After production, we will:

  • Weigh and measure the finished package
  • Provide you a shipping quote via email
  • Send a separate payment link for the shipping fee
  • Ship within 1 business day of shipping fee payment

Typical express shipping costs range from approximately USD $20–$60 depending on package weight and destination. Large or heavy orders may cost more.

4. Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number via email. You can also track your order at any time on our Order Tracking page using your order number and email address.

5. Customs & Import Duties

We ship from Shenzhen, China. International shipments may be subject to customs duties, VAT, or import taxes imposed by your country. These charges are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in our pricing. PCBAForge is not responsible for delays caused by customs clearance.

6. Component Shipment (Customer-Supplied Materials)

If you are supplying your own components, please ship them to our facility at:

Ligao Industrial Park, Longgang, Shenzhen, Guangdong, China

Please include your order number on the package. Contact us via WhatsApp or email before shipping to confirm the exact address and to arrange receipt of your components.

7. Damaged or Lost Shipments

If your package arrives damaged or is lost in transit, please contact us within 7 days of the delivery date (or expected delivery date). We will work with the carrier to file a claim and arrange a replacement or refund as appropriate.

8. Contact

For shipping questions, contact us at admin@pcbaforge.com or via WhatsApp.
