// Frequently Asked Questions
What is the minimum order quantity?
Our minimum is 5 PCBs. We specialize in small batches from 5 to a few hundred units — ideal for prototypes and pre-production runs.
How long does production take?
Standard lead time is 7–10 business days from payment confirmation, including PCB fabrication, SMT/DIP assembly, and functional testing. Express options may be available.
What files do I need to provide?
For PCB fabrication: Gerber files. For assembly: Gerber + BOM (bill of materials) + centroid/placement file. Pack everything in a ZIP and upload on the inquiry page.
Do you do functional testing?
Yes — our engineer will power up and verify each board against your test specification. This is available as an add-on for Turnkey orders. Request a quote →
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept PayPal (with buyer protection), Apple Pay, and Google Pay. Payment is requested after engineer review and quote confirmation — not upfront.
Do you ship worldwide?
Yes. We ship via DHL, FedEx, and other carriers worldwide. Shipping is quoted separately after production, based on weight and destination.
