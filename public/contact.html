Frequently Asked
What files do I need to submit?
Pack your Gerber files, BOM (Bill of Materials), and component placement files into a single ZIP or RAR archive. We accept .zip, .rar, and .7z formats. DFM review is free.
What is the minimum order quantity?
Our minimum is 5 pieces for PCB fabrication and SMT assembly. We specialize in prototype and small batch runs — no need to order hundreds of boards.
How long does production take?
Standard lead time is 7–10 business days for PCB + SMT. Turnkey with component sourcing is typically 10–15 days. Express options are available — ask us.
Do you supply components?
Yes. We offer both CMS (customer-supplied materials) and Turnkey (we source all components). Turnkey orders require a complete BOM with manufacturer part numbers.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay for online payments. Wire transfer is available for larger orders. Payment is made after engineer quote confirmation.
How does functional testing work?
Our engineer performs power-on and functional verification per your test specification. For complex boards, we join you remotely to validate and debug together before shipping.
